01 / Research

Biological questions.
Computational approaches.

My work has grown from studying variation in crop genomes to developing the methods and software that make it interpretable.

a.

Population genomics & quantitative genetics

Large-scale variant discovery, population structure, haplotype analysis and trait mapping. In wheat, my work has included the Watkins landrace collection, LD-based haplotype workflows, TagSNP design, and NAM imputation and GWAS.

b.

From genes to phenotypes

Connecting genetic variation to traits in wheat and pea. My pea research has included haplotype analyses of Mendel’s classical traits and the integration of transcriptome, methylation and RT-PCR evidence.

c.

AI for biology & agentic science Exploring

I’m interested in how AI can support biological research, and how agentic systems might connect scientific literature, analysis tools and computational workflows. This is the next direction I’m exploring from a grounding in genomics and scientific software.

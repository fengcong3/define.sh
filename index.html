01 / Research

Selected
work.

  1. Nature2025Co-first author

    Genomic and genetic insights into Mendel’s pea genes ↗

    My contribution

    I co-led genomics pipelines and haplotype–phenotype analyses to identify genes and alleles underlying Mendel’s pea traits.

    My work also integrated transcriptome, methylation and RT-PCR evidence for gene discovery.

    Read the paper ↗Explore the data ↗
    Authors & citation

    Cong Feng, Baizhi Chen, Julie Hofer, Yan Shi, Mei Jiang, Bo Song, Hong Cheng, et al.

    23 April 2025 · DOI: 10.1038/s41586-025-08891-6

  2. Nature202401 → 02

    Harnessing landrace diversity empowers wheat breeding ↗

    My contribution

    I co-led variant discovery for 1,051 Watkins accessions and developed LD-based haplotype and NAM-GWAS workflows.

    Population structure, NAM imputation and TagSNP design.

    Watkins & Worldwide Wheat G2B ↗
    Authors & citation

    Shifeng Cheng, Cong Feng, Luzie U. Wingen, Hong Cheng, Andrew B. Riche, Mei Jiang, Michelle Leverington-Waite, et al.

    632, 823–831 · DOI: 10.1038/s41586-024-07682-9

  3. Frontiers in Plant Science202202 → 03

    HAPPE: population haplotypes in editable Excel tables ↗

    My contribution

    I developed HAPPE to make population haplotypes easy to analyse, visualise and share in editable Excel tables.

    Scientific software I developed and maintain.

    Explore the source code ↗
    Authors & citation

    HAPPE: a tool for population haplotype analysis and visualization in editable Excel tables.

    Cong Feng, Xingwei Wang, Shishi Wu, Weidong Ning, Bo Song, Jianbin Yan and Shifeng Cheng.

    13, 927407 · DOI: 10.3389/fpls.2022.927407
